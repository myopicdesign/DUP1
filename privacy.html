Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how we process personal data when you use DUPY, including the DUPY website, free online tools, desktop application, and related services.

DUPY is a software tool designed to help users structure, develop, and write video scripts.

For privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact us at: [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS]

02

Personal data we collect

We aim to collect and process only the information reasonably necessary to operate and improve DUPY. Depending on how you use DUPY, we may process the following categories of information.

A. Information you provide

This may include:

  • your email address if you contact us for support or other communications;
  • information you voluntarily provide through forms or other interactions with the website;
  • information provided when purchasing DUPY Pro;
  • information included in support requests or other communications you send to us.

DUPY does not require you to create an account to use the desktop application.

B. Technical and usage data

When you visit the DUPY website or use online features, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as:

  • IP address;
  • browser type and version;
  • operating system and device information;
  • user-agent information;
  • approximate location derived from IP address;
  • pages or features accessed;
  • timestamps and usage information;
  • diagnostic, error, and performance information.

This information may be used to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

C. Script and project content

DUPY is designed so that your projects and scripts can be stored locally on your device.

DUPY does not maintain a cloud database of your scripts or projects.

When using DUPY's local desktop functionality, your scripts, projects, and related files are saved on your device and remain under your control. We do not access, collect, or store these locally saved files unless a specific online feature requires content to be transmitted for processing.

03

Local storage and user files

DUPY is designed around local file storage. Projects created with the desktop application are saved locally by the user. DUPY does not automatically upload your projects to our servers or maintain online copies of them.

You are responsible for managing, backing up, and protecting files stored on your own device.

Deleting a DUPY project from your device does not require any action from DUPY because DUPY does not maintain a separate cloud copy of that project.

04

Online features and content processing

Some DUPY features may require information or content to be transmitted to our servers or third-party service providers in order to perform the requested function.

For example, online tools may process content submitted by you to provide transcription, analysis, scoring, text generation, or other requested functionality.

When you use such a feature, the content you submit may be temporarily processed by DUPY and/or the third-party providers required to provide that feature.

We do not use submitted content to build a personal profile of you. Where practical, online content is processed only for the purpose of providing the functionality you requested.

05

AI and third-party service providers

Certain DUPY features may use third-party artificial intelligence or other technology providers to process content and generate results.

When an AI-powered feature is used, the information necessary to perform your request may be transmitted to the relevant provider. Depending on the feature, this may include:

  • text you submit;
  • script content;
  • prompts or instructions;
  • content generated during the request;
  • technical information necessary to process the request.

Third-party providers process this information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

You should not submit confidential, highly sensitive, or personally identifying information into DUPY's AI-powered features unless you are comfortable with that information being transmitted to the relevant service provider.

DUPY does not use your locally stored scripts or projects to train its own AI models.

06

Free online tools

DUPY may provide free online tools that allow you to submit content for purposes such as video transcription, narrative analysis, structure mapping, or scoring.

If you use an online tool, the content you submit may need to be transmitted to DUPY or third-party service providers in order to provide the requested result.

These online tools are different from the local desktop application. The fact that DUPY Pro can operate locally does not mean that every online DUPY feature operates entirely offline.

We do not permanently store submitted content unless this is specifically necessary for the operation, security, or legal requirements of the relevant service.

07

Payments and purchases

If you purchase DUPY Pro or another paid product, payment information is processed by our third-party payment provider.

We do not receive or store your full payment card number, security code, or other full payment credentials.

The payment provider may collect information such as:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • billing information;
  • payment information;
  • transaction details;
  • purchase and subscription information, where applicable.

The payment provider processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may receive limited information necessary to confirm and manage your purchase, such as your email address, transaction identifier, product purchased, and payment status.
