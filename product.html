What exactly is DUPY Pro? ▾
DUPY Pro is a desktop-first script builder designed to help creators think, structure, write, and refine short-form video scripts. Instead of working in a traditional text editor, you build your script on a node-based canvas, where every part of your video can be moved, edited, scored, and organized independently.

DUPY Pro includes an AI text generator. AI is not built directly into the software, but is managed through a BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) system. You can generate a free API key through Groq and add it to DUPY to generate text directly inside the app. You also get access to numerous pre-built narrative structures developed as node systems, the ability to add visual references and notes to each section, real-time video duration tracking, and local project saving.

No subscription. No cloud. No account required.
Is it really a one-time payment? ▾
Yes. For now, DUPY Pro is a one-time purchase with no recurring subscription.

Pay once and keep using the version you purchased. No monthly fees, no annual plans, and no account required to keep working on your projects.
Does it work offline? ▾
Yes. DUPY Pro is designed to run locally on your computer. Your scripts and projects are saved locally, so you don't need to upload your work to the cloud or stay connected to the internet to use the core features.

Some AI-powered features require an internet connection to generate content.
What platforms is DUPY Pro available on? ▾
DUPY Pro is a desktop application designed for creators who want a dedicated writing environment directly on their computer.

It is currently available for macOS and Windows.
How is DUPY Pro different from the free tool? ▾
The free tool lets you analyze the narrative structures used by other creators, helping you understand how successful videos are structured and replicate their framework.

DUPY Pro is the actual script builder: it lets you build, organize, and manage your own scripts through a node-based system, with pre-built narrative structures, AI generation, visual references, notes, and local project management.

In short: the free tool helps you understand the structures behind successful videos. DUPY Pro lets you build them.
